Upper Limay River

A legendary Patagonian river flowing out of Nahuel Huapi Lake, known for its clear water, varied structure, and strong wild trout. It is the main river of the national park.

The Upper Limay offers classic fly fishing with dry flies, nymphs, and streamers, set against wide-open landscapes and big Patagonian skies.

  • Species: Brown trout, rainbow trout
  • Style: Versatile river fishing, great all season
  • Highlights: Iconic steppe scenery
  • Travel Time: 30–45 minutes from Bariloche
  • Best Time: November to May

Manso River

A pristine mountain river flowing through forests and deep valleys inside Nahuel Huapi National Park.

The Manso is perfect for anglers seeking crystal-clear water, technical fishing, and a true sense of wilderness.

  • Species: Brown trout, rainbow trout
  • Style: Technical river fishing
  • Highlights: Protected waters and untouched surroundings
  • Travel Time: 1–1.30 hours from Bariloche
  • Best Time: November to January

Pichileufú River

An Easy and Elegant Introduction to Fly Fishing in Patagonia

The Pichileufú River is one of the best places in Patagonia to begin your fly fishing journey. Its clear water, gentle current, and well-defined channels create a calm and approachable environment.

Fish are often visible, helping new anglers understand where to cast, how to read the water, and how trout respond to a fly.

Perfect for learning:
  • Basic casting techniques
  • Simple presentations
  • Fly selection and drift control
  • Species: Brown trout, rainbow trout
  • Style: Dry fly fishing, suitable for all levels
  • Best Time: November to February
  • Travel Time: 1 hour from Bariloche

Lake Mascardi

A large Andean lake surrounded by forests and mountains, offering a unique stillwater fly fishing experience near Bariloche.

Known for powerful trout and changing conditions, combining scenic beauty with exciting fishing from shore or boat.

  • Species: Brown trout, rainbow trout, brook trout
  • Style: Lake fishing with streamers and dry flies
  • Highlights: Dramatic scenery and strong fish
  • Best Time: November to January
  • Travel Time: 45 minutes from Bariloche
