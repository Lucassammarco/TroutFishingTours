About Us

Local Guides. Protected Waters. Authentic Patagonia.

At Trout Fishing Tours Patagonia, we are a team of local professional fly fishing guides based in Bariloche, dedicated to sharing the rivers and lakes of northern Patagonia in a responsible, personal, and authentic way.

We live here, fish these waters year-round, and guide with deep respect for the landscapes that make Patagonia one of the world’s most iconic fly fishing destinations.

Where We Guide

Our Home: Northern Patagonia

Our guiding operations are centered around Nahuel Huapi National Park and surrounding waters, a region defined by protected rivers, Andean lakes, native forests, and wild trout.

Fishing inside and around a national park requires more than local knowledge — it demands professionalism, responsibility, and a strong conservation ethic.
